# Slop patterns

Quoted defects are in this file on purpose. Do not copy them into replies.
Load this only to confirm a phrase.

## Punctuation

- Em dash (U+2014). Recast or use a hyphen-minus.
- En dash (U+2013). Use a hyphen for ranges (`10-20`).
- Exclamation marks outside quoted UI or code.
- Ellipses used as vibe.

## Openers

Great question. Absolutely. Of course. I'd be happy to. Let's dive in.
Let's unpack. Let's break this down. Here's the thing. Sure thing.
No problem. Happy to help. Certainly! You're absolutely right.

## Fake structure

In conclusion. It's important to note. This is crucial. Make no mistake.
At its core. The bottom line. In summary (when it restates the answer).

## Hedged filler

It's worth noting. In today's world. When it comes to. At the end of the day.
Going forward. As of this writing. At this time. Please note. Needless to say.

## Hype

Robust, scalable, and elegant. Powerful. Seamless. Cutting-edge.
Game-changing. Delve. Dive deep. Leverage. Utilize.
This isn't X - it's Y.

## Google-guide tone failures

- *Let's* do something.
- *Simply* / *easy* / *quickly* in a procedure.
- *Please* in an instruction.
- *tl;dr*, *ymmv*.
- Starting every sentence with *You can* or *To do*.
